About this notebook

Research,

I’m interested in how the structure of a city relates to environmental exposure and everyday experience—and how to study those relationships with spatial data.

This site brings together ongoing pipelines, prototypes, and research directions. The project notes separate current checkpoints from open questions, with the Clark Kerr explorer as an interactive view into the work.
