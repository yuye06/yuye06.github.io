Would you walk here?

Examining how open vision-language models judge street scenes, how stable those judgments are, and whose perceptions they resemble.

→
Model judgments & perturbations
→
Consistency + group agreement

The question

When models judge a street as safer or more beautiful, which human judgments do they reproduce—and how consistent are their answers?

Approach

  1. Compare paired model judgments with Place Pulse crowd assessments.
  2. Test sensitivity to image order, mirroring, wording, and response format.
  3. Use SPECS to examine perception differences across groups and countries.
  4. Explore whether persona prompts shift a model toward a group's judgments.

Current checkpoint

The project notes document two studies, model-run outputs, analysis scripts, and figures. The public note describes the evaluation design; it does not claim that model judgments are a universal measure of street quality.

Scope & interpretation. Human perception differs across people and contexts. Robustness and group agreement are separate evaluation questions, and persona prompts require careful interpretation.

Next questions

  • Review consistency and group-agreement results together.
  • Document the assumptions behind any interpretation of model-human agreement.
