Urban climate / predictive modeling

Model prototypeResearch note / November 2026

Learning the daily rhythm of urban heat

Exploring a mixture-of-experts framework that brings Earth-observation, LiDAR-derived urban form, and regional weather context into hourly air-temperature mapping.

Surface representations + urban form
→
Weather-conditioned expert routing
→
Hourly temperature estimates

The question

Can complementary representations of urban surfaces and atmospheric conditions improve fine-scale heat mapping across the day and across cities?

Approach

  1. Align Earth-observation representations and LiDAR-derived urban-form features.
  2. Condition a mixture-of-experts router on regional weather, location, and time.
  3. Develop parameter-efficient adaptation and a weather-conditioned decoder.
  4. Plan station-based and cross-city checks alongside interpretation of expert routing.

Current checkpoint

Available repository notes document an implemented architecture, data preparation, and early end-to-end training. The recorded prototype includes stand-in components, so this note does not present the training snapshots as validated cross-city performance.

Scope & interpretation. Air-temperature prediction is distinct from radiant exposure. Generalization, uncertainty, and the contribution of each model stream still need explicit evaluation.

Next questions

  • Complete and verify the weather-context components.
  • Evaluate held-out observations and transfer across study regions.
  • Examine whether routing patterns are interpretable across the diurnal cycle.
