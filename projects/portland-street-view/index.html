Reading Portland from the street

A road-based imagery and segmentation workflow for tracking the sky, vegetation, and built environment visible from Portland streets.

Road network + spatial sampling
→
Panorama selection & segmentation
→
Sky / vegetation / building ratios

The question

How can repeatable street-level sampling support comparisons of sky openness and visible greenery across capture years?

Approach

  1. Use OpenStreetMap roads and sampling points to organize coverage.
  2. Select panoramas by capture year and spatial sampling units.
  3. Segment imagery into classes such as sky, vegetation, and buildings.
  4. Summarize pixel ratios for sky-view and greenery analysis.

Current checkpoint

The project notes document processed 2023–2025 imagery. Acquisition endpoints and known sampling gaps need review before the time series is extended. No Street View imagery is distributed through this site.

Scope & interpretation. Visible pixel ratios depend on sampling, camera context, segmentation, and capture dates. Source access conditions must be respected when rebuilding imagery collections.

Next questions

  • Update the acquisition route and assess geographic sampling gaps.
  • Check comparability across capture years and segmentation outputs.
