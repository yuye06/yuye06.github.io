How open is the urban sky?

A research direction comparing LiDAR- and street-view-derived sky view factor for urban microclimate and resilience studies.

The question

Where do 3D geometry and street-level imagery agree about sky openness, and what explains their differences?

Approach

  1. Develop comparable measures of sky openness from LiDAR and street-level imagery.
  2. Organize geometry and image-derived estimates on a shared spatial basis.
  3. Investigate how observation context affects the comparison.

Current checkpoint

The available repository description establishes the comparison objective. This public note presents the research direction rather than a completed empirical comparison.

Scope & interpretation. Different observation positions, acquisition dates, and definitions of visibility can affect apparent agreement.

Next questions

  • Specify the paired observation and evaluation design.
  • Document uncertainty in each measurement route before interpreting differences.
