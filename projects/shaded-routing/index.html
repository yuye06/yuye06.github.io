Routes that account for heat and shade

A proposed multimodal, preference-conditioned reinforcement-learning framework for personalized shaded route planning, with a focus on children.

→
Preference-conditioned planning
→
Shade / exposure trade-offs

The question

How could route choice account for heat, shade, and differing preferences instead of optimizing distance alone?

Approach

  1. Formulate route planning around environmental context and user preferences.
  2. Explore a multimodal, preference-conditioned learning framework.
  3. Plan evaluations of shade and heat-related route trade-offs.

Current checkpoint

The repository currently describes the framework concept. This is a research proposal, not a deployed navigation service or a validated heat-safety recommendation.

Scope & interpretation. Environmental estimates, accessibility constraints, and the needs of intended users require careful validation before practical route recommendations.

Next questions

  • Define the data, objectives, constraints, and evaluation protocol.
  • Assess preference and exposure trade-offs before testing a routing system.
