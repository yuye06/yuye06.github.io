Green infrastructure and emotional resilience

A replication project examining green infrastructure and urban emotional resilience through double machine learning and interpretable modeling.

The question

How can observational urban data support a more careful assessment of green infrastructure and emotional resilience during disruption?

Approach

  1. Construct resilience measures for Shanghai neighborhoods during the 2022 lockdown.
  2. Use double machine learning for the causal-estimation workflow.
  3. Explore interpretation with MLP-SHAP and resilience metrics.

Current checkpoint

The public repository describes replication code for the study 'Building urban emotional resilience through green infrastructure: causal evidence from double machine learning.' This site summarizes the research scope rather than reproducing the study's findings.

Scope & interpretation. Causal interpretation depends on the identification assumptions and measurement choices of the study design.

Next questions

  • Keep replication steps and assumptions transparent.
  • Assess robustness and interpretation within the study's scope.
