Chapters and captions

  1. Schooly. From campus evidence to community action. A silent walkthrough of the working demo prototype: where shade reaches the ground students use, and how tree placement changes it.
  2. Schooly is listed with the other research projects at yuye06.github.io/projects.
  3. The project note states the question, the approach and what the demo does and does not show.
  4. An invented campus: buildings in gray, student-use zones in blue, existing trees in green.
  5. Shade is computed in the browser from the sun's position for 15 August at 35.1° N, on a 1 m grid.

  15. Existing campus: 0.04 h of zone shade per cell; 97% of zone ground is never shaded at the sampled hours.
  16. Preset: 10 proposed trees (dashed) inside the student-use zones.
  17. Zone shade rises from 0.04 h to 0.89 h per cell, and never-shaded ground falls to 79%.
  18. At 15:00 the same trees cast longer shadows across the zones.
  19. Preset: the same 10 trees along the campus edge instead.
  20. Zone shade stays at 0.04 h per cell: on this campus the edge trees' shadows almost never reach student-use ground.
  21. At 15:00 the edge trees' shadows still fall mostly outside the zones.
  22. Same 10 trees, different placement. Zone shade-hours per cell: existing 0.04 h; 10 trees inside the zones 0.89 h; 10 trees at the campus edge 0.04 h. Values describe this invented campus only.
  23. Trees can also be placed by clicking on the plan and removed by clicking them; every value is recalculated.
  24. The method and its limits are on the page: simplified crowns, clear sky, one date; not SOLWEIG, Tmrt or UTCI.
  25. Planned, not yet built. Community step: example feedback from staff and families, grouped by topic and correctable by reviewers. Decision step: an evidence-linked brief with a human approval state. No external action is taken. Research-derived New Mexico campus layers only after a data-release review.
