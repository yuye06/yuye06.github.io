How this is calculated

Sun altitude and azimuth come from standard solar-geometry formulas for 15 August at 35.1°N, 106.6°W, at the nine whole hours 08:00–16:00 MDT, clear sky. The campus is a 1 m grid. A cell is in building shade if a line toward the sun meets a building below its roof height; it is in tree shade if it falls inside a crown's projected shadow (crown treated as a disk at mid-crown height). Shade-hours sum the nine hourly samples, each counted as one hour, averaged over student-use cells.

Not included: terrain, diffuse light, crown porosity, tree growth and survival, irrigation, weather, actual student schedules or locations, and any thermal-comfort quantity. Results describe this invented geometry only.
